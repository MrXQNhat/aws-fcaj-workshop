**On this page**, you will need to introduce your worklog. **How** did you complete it? How many weeks did you take to complete the program? **What** did you do in those weeks?

Typically, and as a standard, a worklog is carried out over about 3 months (throughout the internship period) with weekly contents as follows:

**Week 1:** [Getting familiar with AWS, IAM, and basic AWS services](1.1-week1/)

**Week 2:** [Researching DRM architectures and Digital Content Protection mechanisms](1.2-week2/)

**Week 3:** [Drafting the SecureLearn Proposal and evaluating the 6 AWS Well-Architected Pillars](1.3-week3/)

**Week 4:** [Designing the complete System Architecture and Data Flow Diagrams for SecureLearn](1.4-week4/)

**Week 5:** [Building a Serverless Document Management System (DMS) with Lambda, DynamoDB, API Gateway, and AWS SAM](1.5-week5/)

**Week 6:** [Implementing event-driven messaging with Amazon SQS/SNS, Docker deployments on EC2/RDS/ECR, and Cognito authentication](1.6-week6/)

**Week 7:** [Developing the SecureLearn Media Pipeline with Cloudflare R2, Workers, and AWS Elemental MediaConvert](1.7-week7/)

**Week 8:** [Integrating JWT authentication, short-lived playback tokens, Isolation Forest AI anomaly detection, and Dynamic Watermarking](1.8-week8/)

**Week 9:** [Cloud Security Fundamentals & Auditing AWS IAM Misconfigurations](1.9-week9/)

**Week 10:** [Cloud Infrastructure Hacking, S3 Exposure, SSRF/IMDS Exploitation, and Serverless Security](1.10-week10/)

**Week 11:** [Container & Kubernetes Security, RBAC Auditing, Pod Breakout, and Runtime Hardening](1.11-week11/)

**Week 12:** [Cloud Incident Response, Threat Hunting with CloudTrail, IaC Security Scanning, and Final Project Review](1.12-week12/)
