# Summary Report: “AWS CLOUD AND AI DAY HANOI”

### Event Objectives

The "AWS Cloud and AI Day Hanoi" event was organized to provide a direct networking space between the Vietnamese technology community and leading experts from AWS and enterprise partners. The event focuses on the following strategic objectives:
- **Update pioneering technology trends**: Introduce cloud solutions, modern data architectures, and especially the explosion of Agentic AI and Generative AI in optimizing operational processes.
- **Hands-on practice and real-world experience**: Provide a practical environment through hands-on workshops, helping developers and engineers directly build and deploy AI Agent solutions right at the event.
- **Community and enterprise networking**: Create a launching pad for exchanges and experience sharing among IT managers, solution architects, and leading technology experts.

### Speakers

The event brought together a team of speakers representing regulatory agencies, technology corporation leaders, and solution experts from AWS:
- **Phạm Đức Long** – Deputy Minister of Information and Communications / Ministry of Science & Technology
- **Jennifer Wicks** – U.S. Ambassador to Vietnam
- **Dr. Werner Vogels** – Chief Technology Officer (CTO), Amazon.com
- **Eric Yeo** – Country General Manager (Malaysia, Vietnam, Indochina), AWS
- **Dr. Jens Lottner** – Chief Executive Officer (CEO), Techcombank
- **Mai Le** – Director of Product Management, Katalon
- Along with top Solutions Architects and Engineers from AWS and partners VPBank, Cloud Kinetics.

### Key Highlights

The program took place with more than 30 in-depth sharing sessions, covering key topics:
#### Keynote: Breaking limits with Agentic AI and AWS 
The opening session focused on the question "What if we turn every idea into reality?". Speakers reviewed how AWS equips tools for modern builders, while listening to success stories from Techcombank and Katalon when bringing AI into their operational core.
#### Transformation and System Modernization with Agentic AI: Technical sessions went deep into applying AI Agents to solve legacy application modernization problems and accelerate the software development process:
  - **Migration at Scale with AWS Transform**: Methods for modernizing large applications and infrastructure, reducing technical debt through automated agents.
  - **Building next-generation applications**: Leveraging Agentic AI to automate the SDLC cycle from planning, coding to maintenance.
#### AI-Ready Data Foundation 
To run AI models effectively, data plays a decisive prerequisite role. The discussion session focused on standardizing data architecture, large-scale data processing workflows, and security to ensure accuracy for AI applications.
### Key Takeaways

Attending the event helped accumulate many new perspectives from both mindset and practical deployment techniques.
#### Architectural & Strategic Mindset
  - **Agentic AI Shift**: Clearly recognized the shift from pure Generative AI (generating content) to Agentic AI (having the capability to self-plan, make decisions, and execute tasks).
  - **Data-First Mindset**: To successfully deploy AI in enterprises, the foundational factor does not lie in the model but in the structure and quality of underlying data.
#### In-depth Techniques:
  - Understood the application modernization process using AWS automation tools, helping shorten system conversion time.
  - Grasped how to set up an Internal Developer Platform (IDP) on EKS to accelerate software delivery speed.
  - Skills to integrate large language models (LLMs) into workflows through the AWS service ecosystem.  

### Applying to Work

The knowledge acquired from the event brings clear application value to current and future projects:
- **Application to Projects & Major Assignments**: Experiment with integrating AI services like Amazon Bedrock/Amazon Q into student software projects to optimize development processes.
- **Cloud & AI Learning Orientation**: Supplement knowledge of Microservices, Serverless, and Kubernetes (EKS) architectures on the AWS platform into the self-study roadmap.
- **Internship Preparation**: Leverage knowledge about Agentic AI and Cloud to enrich the capability profile (CV), preparing for upcoming internship opportunities.

### Event Experience

The "AWS Cloud and AI Day Hanoi" event was a learning journey that brought many emotional and practical values. Not stopping at theoretical presentations, the event brought an energetic innovative atmosphere.
#### Impressions on Scale and Speakers
The presence of big names like CTO Werner Vogels along with representatives from Ministries and large banks affirmed the importance of the Cloud/AI technology wave in Vietnam. How speakers conveyed real business problems made the technology picture extremely close and rich in strategic value.
#### Impressions on Professional Content and AI Trends 
Technical sharing sessions were designed from basic to advanced levels, very suitable for students to follow and take notes.
- **Approaching Agentic AI trends**: Through presentations like "Building software like never before with agentic AI", I visualized more clearly how programmers will work in the near future when combining with AI Agents.
- **Time Saving & Proactive Absorption**: Participating remotely helped me flexibly choose to follow sharing sessions most suitable for my personal learning orientation.
#### Watch Party & Interactive Environment 
At the online connection point in Ho Chi Minh City, I still felt the heat from the main event in Hanoi.
- **Discussing with friends**: Exchanging and discussing demonstrated solutions with fellow students and the AWS community in Ho Chi Minh City.
- **Inspiring learning**: The event helped me realize the importance of proactively learning new technologies like Cloud & AI right while sitting in school chairs to avoid falling behind.
Summary: The event not only brought an updated volume of technical knowledge but also strongly inspired innovative thinking, helping me become more confident in proposing and deploying modern AI & Cloud solutions into practical work.
